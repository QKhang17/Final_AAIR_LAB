01 Data Collection Scope

1. Information We Collect

AAIR collects personal and system-level metadata strictly necessary to operate our academic platform, authenticate verified researchers, provide reproducible code repositories, and maintain digital infrastructure integrity.

  • check_circle Full Name & Title: Used for lab correspondence and research author listings.
  • check_circle Institutional Email: Verification for university, lab, or industry accounts.
  • check_circle Organization / Institution: Affiliation records for collaborative grant work.
  • check_circle Inquiries & Messages: Records submitted via support and collaboration forms.

  • check_circle Usage Metrics: Model benchmark executions, paper downloads, API endpoint queries.

03 Browser Storage

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal cookies and browser local storage strictly required for platform operation, state preservation in visual demos, and essential security tokens.

lock Authentication

Validates your authenticated session when accessing private testbeds.

settings Preferences

Remembers UI settings like code font size, syntax theme, or tab layouts.

analytics Basic Analytics

Anonymized telemetry without cross-site tracking or third-party ad profiling.

cookie

Controlling Your Cookie Preferences

Your browser controls allow you to disable or remove cookies. While most of the public academic content remains fully accessible with cookies disabled, session persistence and interactive code notebooks may experience reduced functionality.

05 System Defense

5. Data Security (Protecting Your Information)

AAIR maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards crafted to protect information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, alteration, or destructive compromise.

key

Access Control

Strict least-privilege role separation. Production model weights and databases require hardware-backed multi-factor authentication.

lock_clock

Technical Safeguards

TLS 1.3 encryption across all network boundaries, salted cryptographic hashing for account records, and containerized computation environments.

monitoring

Active Monitoring

Continuous anomalous intrusion analysis, rate-limit enforcement, and automated static vulnerability scanning across our open codebase.

info

Institutional Notice: While we adhere to rigorous academic engineering best practices, no digital storage or transmission channel is completely invulnerable. In the event of a critical security disclosure, we coordinate rapid response pursuant to incident response protocol.

06 Lifecycle Schedule

6. Data Retention

AAIR retains personal data only for as long as reasonably required to fulfill the objectives described in this policy, honor scientific peer-review verifiability, resolve disputes, or satisfy public grant record-keeping obligations.

Data Lifecycle Trajectory
STAGE 01 Collection User registration & explicit lab inquiry submission.
STAGE 02 Active Use Session verification, paper peer review & compute sandbox.
STAGE 03 Storage Archived in encrypted cold storage per grant compliance.
STAGE 04 Deletion Cryptographic wiping or anonymization upon request.
07 User Empowerment

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction and the legal jurisdiction governing your interaction with AAIR, you may hold several procedural rights regarding your stored identifiers:

inventory_2
Access & Export:

Request a readable digital archive of your personal credentials and associated activity records.

edit_square
Rectification & Correction:

Amend inaccurate institutional affiliations, misspelled researcher names, or updated contact points.

delete_forever
Deletion (Right to be Forgotten):

Request permanent erasure of non-public accounts, subject to irrevocable DOI scientific citations.

block
Consent Revocation:

Withdraw authorization previously granted for academic newsletter digests or survey participation.

Note: Procedural rights may vary in accordance with applicable regional privacy frameworks (such as GDPR, CCPA, or institutional university policies).
