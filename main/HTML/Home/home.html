FOUNDATIONAL MISSION & SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLES

Human Expertise Belongs in the Machine Learning Loop.

High-stakes financial document intelligence demands absolute factual fidelity. At AAIR Lab, we develop hybrid human-in-the-loop architectures where advanced multi-modal models are continuously calibrated, verified, and audited against ground-truth corporate evidence.

Scientific Mission

To pioneer transparent, explainable document AI architectures that seamlessly integrate domain knowledge with foundation models, eliminating hallucinations in critical workflows.

Research Vision

To engineer open, high-fidelity benchmark datasets (e.g., VN-CorpBench, FinIE-LLM) that empower academic and industry researchers worldwide to evaluate model robustness.

Active Research Thrusts

Multi-Modal Document Understanding (VLM) • Financial Knowledge Graphs & Ontological Grounding • Inter-Annotator Agreement Dynamics • Automated Audit Verification & Fraud Detection.
